Clinical Programs

Areas of Diabetes Care

Personalized treatment protocols covering all aspects of diabetes management and complication prevention.

Type 1 Diabetes Mellitus

Individualized diabetes management, insulin therapy support, blood glucose monitoring, nutritional guidance, and education to help patients manage their condition safely and effectively.

Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus

Personalized treatment plans incorporating appropriate medication, lifestyle modification, healthy eating, physical activity, weight management, and regular monitoring of blood glucose levels.

Gestational Diabetes Mellitus (GDM)

Care for women diagnosed with diabetes during pregnancy, with emphasis on maternal blood glucose control, nutritional counselling, appropriate monitoring, and reducing risks to both mother and baby. Follow-up after delivery is also important to assess future diabetes risk and support long-term metabolic health.

Prediabetes & Diabetes Prevention

Early identification of individuals at increased risk of developing diabetes, with practical guidance on nutrition, physical activity, weight management, and lifestyle changes to help delay or prevent the onset of Type 2 diabetes.

Insulin Therapy & Glucose Monitoring

Guidance on appropriate insulin use, glucose monitoring, treatment adherence, and individualized strategies to improve glycaemic control.

Diabetes-Related Complications & Foot Care

Assessment and risk reduction for complications affecting the eyes, kidneys, nerves, heart, blood vessels, and feet, with appropriate screening and specialist referral when needed.

Diabetes in Older Adults and Complex Cases

Individualized treatment planning that considers other medical conditions, medication safety, risk of hypoglycaemia, functional status, and the patient’s overall health and quality of life.

Patient-Centred Philosophy

A Patient-Centred Approach

Every person experiences diabetes differently. Dr. Nath emphasizes clear communication, patient education, practical lifestyle advice, evidence-based treatment, and regular follow-up. His goal is to help patients understand their condition, participate actively in their treatment, and achieve sustainable improvements in their health.

Patient Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions About Diabetes

Clear answers to common questions about insulin dosage, diet, and chamber consultations.

1. Is insulin therapy required for life once started for diabetes control?
For Type 2 Diabetes, blood glucose can often be stabilized through lifestyle modifications, weight loss, and modern oral medications, allowing insulin dosage to be tapered under medical supervision. However, for Type 1 Diabetes and Gestational Diabetes (pregnancy), insulin remains essential as advised by Dr. Rajib Deb Nath.
2. What immediate action should be taken during a sudden low blood sugar event (Hypoglycemia)?
If your blood sugar drops below 4.0 mmol/L or you experience sweating, tremors, or confusion, immediately follow the 15-15 Rule: consume 15 grams of fast-acting carbohydrates (such as 3-4 glucose biscuits or half a glass of fruit juice), wait 15 minutes, and re-check your blood glucose level.
3. What medical reports should I bring for my first chamber appointment?
Please bring all recent prescriptions from the last 3 months, your latest Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS) and 2-Hour Post Breakfast (2h ABF) readings, your most recent HbA1c test report, and Kidney Function (Serum Creatinine) test results for an effective consultation.
4. How often should HbA1c and diabetic complication screenings be performed?
HbA1c tests should ideally be performed every 3 to 6 months to monitor long-term blood sugar stability. Comprehensive organ screenings—including Diabetic Nephropathy (Kidneys), Retinopathy (Eyes), and Foot Examinations—are recommended annually.
