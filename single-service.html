Q & A

General Medicine FAQ

What should I bring for my first General Medicine consultation?
Please bring all your previous medical prescriptions, recent lab test reports (blood tests, ultrasonogram, ECG), and a list of any daily medications you are currently taking.
Do I need to fast before visiting for routine health checkups?
If you plan to complete fasting blood sugar (FBS) or lipid profile tests on the same day, 8-10 hours of overnight fasting is recommended. For general consultation, fasting is not required.
How can I book an urgent chamber serial?
You can click the 'Book Appointment' button on this website or call our chamber serial hotline (+8801325109870) directly for serial allocation.
