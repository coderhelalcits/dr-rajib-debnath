Data Confidentiality & Security

Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting patient confidentiality, medical history privacy, and personal data security

Home > Privacy Policy
1

Information We Collect

When you book a chamber serial or contact our medical chamber, we collect relevant patient information necessary for clinical registration and appointment scheduling, including:

  • Patient Full Name, Age, and Contact Mobile Number.
  • Brief Medical Symptom Description or Specialization requested (e.g. Diabetes, General Medicine, Obesity & Endocrine Care).
  • Preferred Chamber Appointment Date & Chamber Location.
2

How We Use Patient Information

Your personal and contact details are used strictly for clinical management purposes:

  • Scheduling chamber appointment serials and sending SMS confirmation/reminder alerts.
  • Maintaining accurate clinical consultation history for follow-up diabetes and medicine treatment plans.
  • Communicating urgent chamber schedule changes or emergency visiting hour adjustments.
3

Strict Doctor-Patient Medical Confidentiality

In accordance with international medical ethics and Bangladesh Medical & Dental Council (BMDC) guidelines, all patient medical records, diagnostic test results, and personal disclosures are kept under strict medical confidentiality. We never sell, rent, or trade patient data to third-party marketing companies or commercial advertisers.

4

Data Protection & Technical Safeguards

We implement administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Access to online serial booking data is restricted solely to authorized chamber administrative personnel.

5

Patient Rights & Data Access

Patients have the right to request review, update, or correction of their personal contact details on record. If you wish to update your appointment contact phone number or cancel stored serial records, you may contact our chamber hotline.
